Every service earns its score across a full week of real dinners — cold Monday nights, midweek fatigue, guests, leftovers, all of it.

1. Plate

Flavor, texture, freshness on day three, sauce integrity, and how it reheats.

2. Fit

Ordering flow, filters, portions, cleanup, and how the meal works on a Tuesday.

Pricing, cancellation, limitations, and best-fit use cases are easy to find.
