About Platter

Chefs as artists.
Food as a relationship.

Platter is a food and wellness publication built on one thesis: chefs are artists and craftspeople, and wellness is your relationship to food — to yourself, to others, to the land, and to the planet.

The thesis

Platter treats chefs as artists and craftspeople. Wellness here is your relationship to food — to yourself, to others, to the land, and to the planet.

How the desk is funded

Independent, on the record.

The editorial desk operates independently — no chef we interview and no dish we publish is chosen to sell a product. Our standards are on the record.

Read the editorial standards
Reader newsletter.

The Briefing is free. When a paid tier ships, every editorial page on this site stays free — the paid tier funds test kitchens and contributor rates.

Reader
No sponsored interviews. Ever.

No chef pays to be interviewed. No restaurant pays for coverage. If we ever run a sponsored piece anywhere on the site, it will be labeled at the top and cannot influence editorial.

Policy
Editorial independence.

Who we interview, what we cook, and what we publish sit with the editor. No advertiser, partner, or sponsor gets a say.
