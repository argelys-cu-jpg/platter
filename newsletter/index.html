FAQ

The obvious questions, answered.

Everything below is what people actually ask before they subscribe. If you want the long version, our editorial standards page has more.

Is it really free?

Yes. All four briefings, forever. We fund the desk with revenue from Batch (our meal planning tool) and a small number of editorial partnerships that never influence the ranking.

How often will you email me?

As often as you choose — one to four times a week. Each briefing lands on its own day. There is no daily digest, no cross-post, no filler send.

Can I change my briefings later?

Any time. Every issue has a one-click link to update your picks or unsubscribe entirely. We do not require an account, a login, or a password reset.

Do you sell or share my email?

No. Not to partners, not to advertisers, not to anyone. We collect the least data we can get away with, and we say so on our editorial standards page.

How do reviews and ranking movements make it in?

Every week we surface one ranking change, one new review, and one movement on the desk. The briefing tells you what changed, why it changed, and whether we would recommend the service to a specific reader — not everyone.
