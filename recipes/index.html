The Platter Kitchen

How every recipe gets tested.

Every recipe on the desk is cooked at least three times before it publishes — Tuesday-night mode, weekend mode, and one deliberately-going-wrong mode.

1. First pass

The cook writes it once, on a real weeknight, with a real week's fatigue.

2. Cross-test

A second cook makes it from the recipe card alone. No calling, no clarifications.

3. Rescue test

We deliberately swap, sub, and shortcut it — and only publish if it still works.
